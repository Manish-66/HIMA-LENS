# Indian Statutory Codes: BIS, IRC, MoRTH & HPPWD
### Reference Document for Technical Writing & Slope Engineering Dossiers

Statutory requirements applicable to Himalayan hill roads and public infrastructure in Himachal Pradesh.

---

## 1. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)

### A. IS:14458 — Retaining Walls for Hill Roads (Parts 1 to 4)
- **Part 1 (Selection of Type of Wall):**
  - Gravity Walls (Masonry/Plum Concrete): Suitable for heights $H \le 6.0\text{ m}$.
  - Gabion Walls: Flexible, free-draining; ideal for differential settlement zones and toe erosion, suitable up to $H \le 8.0\text{ m}$.
  - Cantilever / Counterfort RCC Walls: Suitable for $H > 6.0\text{ m}$ on competent rock foundations.
  - Reinforced Soil (RS) Walls / Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE): Suitable for high embankments on firm strata.
- **Part 2 (Design of Retaining / Breast Walls):**
  - Minimum Base Width ($B$): Typically $0.4H$ to $0.6H$ for gravity walls on rock; $0.6H$ to $0.8H$ on soil/debris.
  - Minimum Front Batter: $1:3$ to $1:6$ for breast walls; $1:12$ to $1:6$ for retaining walls.
  - Weep Holes: Minimum $100\text{ mm}$ internal diameter perforated pipes or PVC sleeves spaced at $1.5\text{ m}$ horizontally and vertically in staggered rows, with an outward downward slope of $1:20$ and wrapped in non-woven geotextile.
  - Filter Backing: Graded gravel/crushed stone filter backing of minimum thickness $300\text{ mm}$ along the entire back face of the wall.
- **Part 3 (Construction Practices):** Foundation excavation minimum depth $1.2\text{ m}$ below scour level or down to hard bedrock; plum placement requirements.
- **Part 4 (Design of Gabion Structures):** Minimum zinc coating $\ge 240\text{ g/m}^2$, double-twisted hexagonal wire mesh (mesh size $100\text{ mm}\times 120\text{ mm}$, wire diameter $\ge 2.7\text{ mm}$ core / $3.7\text{ mm}$ with PVC coating).

### B. IS:1893 (Part 1) — Seismic Design Criteria
- Himachal Pradesh lies entirely within **Seismic Zone IV and Zone V** (Very High Damage Risk Zone).
- Zone factor $Z = 0.24$ (Zone IV) to $0.36$ (Zone V).
- Pseudo-static horizontal seismic acceleration coefficient $\alpha_h = \frac{Z}{2} \cdot \frac{S_a}{g} \cdot \left(\frac{I}{R}\right)$.
- Vertical seismic coefficient $\alpha_v = \frac{2}{3}\alpha_h$.

## 2. Indian Roads Congress (IRC)

### A. IRC:SP:48 — Hill Road Manual
- **Carriageway & Formation:** Single lane ($3.75\text{ m}$ carriageway, $6.0\text{ m}$ formation), Double lane ($7.0\text{ m}$ carriageway, $8.8\text{ m}-10.0\text{ m}$ formation).
- **Hill Side Drains:** Paved saucer drains or L-shaped drains ($0.6\text{ m}-0.9\text{ m}$ width) with minimum longitudinal gradient of $1.5\%$ towards culvert inlets.
- **Catchwater Drains:** Located at minimum $3.0\text{ m}$ to $5.0\text{ m}$ uphill from the outer edge of the cutting scarp. Trapezoidal cross-section lined with $100\text{ mm}$ dry stone masonry or cement concrete ($1:3:6$) to prevent leakage into the slope face.
- **Cross Drainage (Culverts):** Minimum pipe culvert internal diameter $900\text{ mm}$ (to prevent mountain debris/boulder clogging), spaced at maximum intervals of $150\text{ m}-200\text{ m}$ along hill roads.

## 3. Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH - 5th Revision)

- **Section 300 (Earthwork, Erosion Control & Drainage):**
  - Benching of cut slopes: Steps of $1.5\text{ m}-2.0\text{ m}$ width at vertical intervals of $5.0\text{ m}-6.0\text{ m}$ with inward cross fall of $1:10$.
  - Hydroseeding with native grass seeds, organic mulch, and tackifiers.
- **Section 1000 & 1100 (Structures & Protection Works):**
  - Plum concrete: Nominal mix $1:3:6$ or $1:2:4$ incorporating clean, sound boulder plums (maximum size $150\text{ mm}$, plums comprising $30\%-40\%$ of total volume).
  - Geotextiles for subsurface drainage: High-permeability needle-punched non-woven polypropylene ($> 200\text{ g/m}^2$, apparent opening size $AOS \le 0.09\text{ mm}$).
