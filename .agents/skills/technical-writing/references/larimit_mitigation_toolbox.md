# LaRiMiT (Landslide Risk Mitigation Toolbox)
### Reference Document for Technical Writing & Slope Engineering Dossiers

Sourced from the Landslide Risk Mitigation Toolbox (LaRiMiT), developed by the Italian National Research Council (CNR-IRPI) and the University of Salerno.

---

## 1. Remediation Classification Architecture

LaRiMiT establishes an internationally recognized decision matrix categorizing slope risk mitigation into two primary strategic domains:

```
LANDSLIDE RISK MITIGATION
├── 1. STRUCTURAL COUNTERMEASURES
│   ├── A. Modifying Slope Geometry (Unloading crest, toe berm counterweight, benching)
│   ├── B. Surface & Subsurface Drainage (Catchwater drains, French drains, deep sub-horizontal drains)
│   ├── C. Retaining & Supporting Structures (Gravity walls, gabions, anchored soldier piles, counterfort walls)
│   ├── D. Internal Slope Reinforcement (Self-drilling soil nails, rock bolts, ground anchors, micropiles)
│   └── E. Surface Protection & Containment (High-tensile wire mesh, flexible ring-net barriers, shotcrete, rockfall sheds)
│
└── 2. NON-STRUCTURAL COUNTERMEASURES
    ├── A. Nature-Based Solutions & Bio-Engineering (Vetiver grass, brush layering, live fascines, hydroseeding)
    ├── B. Monitoring & Early Warning Systems (MEMS tiltmeters, vibrating wire piezometers, rainfall thresholds)
    ├── C. Land-Use Planning & Hazard Zonation (Setback limits, building bans in red zones per IS:14680)
    └── D. Emergency Preparedness & Traffic Protocols (Pre-monsoon diversions, SOP alert triggers, temporary lane closures)
```

## 2. Countermeasure Suitability by Movement Type

| Kinematic Failure Type | Preferred Structural Countermeasure | Recommended Drainage & Protection | Less Effective / Counterproductive |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rock Fall / Topple** | High-tensile pinned wire mesh (Tecco), rock bolt dowels, flexible ring-net barriers (500-3000 kJ). | Ditch along road toe with gravel cushion; catch fence. | Gravity retaining wall at road level without crest catchment. |
| **Translational Debris Slide** | Staggered soil nailing grid with reinforced shotcrete or flexible grillage; gabion toe wall. | Sub-horizontal perforated drains (5% upward grade); lined crest catchwater drain. | Unanchored dry stone masonry walls; unlined catchwater drains. |
| **Rotational Debris Slump** | Plum concrete or cantilever RCC retaining wall founded on solid rock; toe counterweight berm. | Deep trench French drains along slip boundary; weep holes with geotextile wrap. | Unloading the toe (cutting road wider at toe accelerates rotation). |
| **Channelized Debris Flow** | Flexible debris-resisting ring-net barrier; reinforced concrete check dams (Sabo dams). | Energy dissipating stepped cascades; deflection spurs directing flow to wide culvert. | Thin masonry walls (punched through by dynamic boulder impact). |
| **Soil Creep / Subsidence** | Micro-piling through shear surface; reinforced soil (MSE) embankment with geogrids. | Horizontal bore drains; deep collector interceptor drains. | Superficial plastering or unreinforced mortar toppings. |

## 3. Design Selection Matrix for Technical Reports

When prescribing countermeasures in Section 8 of the HPPWD Preliminary Project Report (PPR):
1. **Primary Structural Intervention:** Must directly address the primary kinematic driving force (e.g., active earth pressure, boulder impact momentum, basal shear slip).
2. **Mandatory Hydraulic Intervention:** Must prevent water ingress and relieve pore pressure (effective stress principle). Never specify a retaining structure without an engineered drainage detail.
3. **Environmental & Surface Stabilization:** Must prevent secondary erosion and weather degradation (bio-engineering, hydroseeding, shotcrete with weep holes).
4. **Maintenance & Monitoring Protocol:** Define inspection cycles (pre-monsoon / post-monsoon per CEDD Geoguide 5) and emergency warning trigger thresholds.
