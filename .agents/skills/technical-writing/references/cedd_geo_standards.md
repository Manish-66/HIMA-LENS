# CEDD Geotechnical Engineering Office (GEO) Standards
### Reference Document for Technical Writing & Slope Engineering Dossiers

Sourced from the Geotechnical Engineering Office (GEO), Civil Engineering and Development Department (CEDD), The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

## 1. Landslip Prevention and Mitigation Programme (LPMitP) Principles

Launched in 2010 as the successor to the Landslip Preventive Measures Programme (LPMP, 1977), the LPMitP is the global benchmark for systematic slope risk reduction.

### A. The ALARP Risk Criterion
- Slope risk must be mitigated to **"As Low As Reasonably Practicable" (ALARP)**.
- **Individual Risk:** Maximum allowable individual risk of fatality $< 10^{-5}$ per year for existing developments and $< 10^{-6}$ per year for new developments.
- **Societal Risk:** Assessed via $F$-$N$ curves (cumulative frequency $F$ of events causing $N$ or more fatalities).

### B. Two-Pronged Strategy
1. **Man-Made Slopes:** Systematic safety screening, preventive maintenance, and structural upgrading using standardized prescriptive measures.
2. **Natural Hillside Catchments:** Mitigation of debris flows and open-hillsides failures using defensive barriers, deflection berms, and retention basins without excessive disturbance to natural vegetation.

## 2. GEO Publication No. 1/2009: Prescriptive Measures for Slopes & Retaining Walls

Prescriptive measures are pre-engineered, standardized, experience-based remediation modules that do not require detailed site-specific numerical back-analysis when specific screening criteria are met.

### A. Soil Nailing Criteria (Section 4)
- **Bar Specification:** High-yield deformed steel rebar complying with BS 4449 (typically Grade 500B, $\phi = 25\text{ mm}$ or $32\text{ mm}$, minimum yield strength $f_y = 500\text{ N/mm}^2$).
- **Drillhole Diameter:** Minimum $100\text{ mm}$ to $150\text{ mm}$ bore diameter.
- **Drillhole Inclination:** Sub-horizontal, typically inclined downward at $10^\circ$ to $20^\circ$ to the horizontal to facilitate cementitious grout encapsulation.
- **Grout Specification:** Non-shrink cementitious grout with minimum 28-day characteristic compressive cube strength $f_{cu} \ge 30\text{ N/mm}^2$, water-cement ratio $0.40$ to $0.45$.
- **Corrosion Protection:**
  - Class 1 (Aggressive / 120-year design life): Corrugated HDPE / uPVC sheathing with minimum $5\text{ mm}$ internal grout cover and $10\text{ mm}$ external grout cover.
  - Class 2 (Standard / 60-year design life): Hot-dip galvanization ($\ge 610\text{ g/m}^2$) plus sacrificial steel thickness allowance.
- **Grid Layout:** Typically staggered diamond or square pattern with horizontal and vertical spacing $S_h, S_v$ between $1.5\text{ m}$ and $2.0\text{ m}$.
- **Nail Head:** Cast-in-situ reinforced concrete pad ($400\text{ mm}\times 400\text{ mm}\times 200\text{ mm}$) or continuous grillage beam tied with high-strength bearing plate and spherical washer.

### B. Sub-surface Drainage (Section 5)
- **Sub-horizontal Raking Drains:**
  - Length: Typically $10\text{ m}$ to $30\text{ m}$, designed to depress the transient perched phreatic water table behind the slope face.
  - Gradient: Minimum upward gradient of $5\%$ ($1:20$) into the slope to ensure self-cleansing gravity discharge.
  - Pipe: Perforated or slotted rigid uPVC / HDPE pipe ($\phi 50\text{ mm}$ to $75\text{ mm}$), wrapped in needle-punched non-woven geotextile filter fabric (Class 1 durability) to prevent piping of fine silt/clay matrix.

## 3. GEO Report No. 299 / Kwan (2012): Flexible Debris-Resisting Barriers

For channelized debris flows and open-hillside debris avalanches:

### A. Kinetic Energy & Impact Load Sizing
- Dynamic boulder impact force calculated via the Hertzian contact or modified puncturing equation:
  $$F_{dyn} = 0.5 \cdot \rho_{debris} \cdot v^2 \cdot \cos\theta \cdot A_{contact} + N_{boulder}$$
- High-tensile steel wire ring nets (tensile strength $1770\text{ N/mm}^2$) capable of absorbing kinetic energy from $500\text{ kJ}$ to $>8000\text{ kJ}$.
- Energy dissipation through friction brake rings installed along upper and lower support cables.

### B. Retention Capacity & Overtopping
- Sized for minimum $1.2\times$ the design debris volume ($\text{m}^3$) anticipated from the upstream catchment.
- Minimum freeboard of $1.0\text{ m}$ above the maximum anticipated debris deposition wedge.
- Bottom clearance ($0.5\text{ m}-1.0\text{ m}$) with coarse steel wire mesh baffle to allow normal hydrological baseflow while arresting coarse gravel and boulders ($> 300\text{ mm}$).

## 4. Geoguide 5: Guide to Slope Practice (Quality Assurance & Inspections)

- **Engineer Inspections:** Mandatory every 5 years for high-consequence Category 1 slopes.
- **Routine Maintenance Inspections (RMI):** Conducted annually before the rainy season (pre-monsoon in India / pre-typhoon in HK).
- **Core Checklist Items:**
  1. Drainage clear of vegetation, silt, and debris blockage.
  2. Weep holes unobstructed and draining freely.
  3. Crack monitoring: Inspect tension cracks along slope crest; seal immediately with bituminous mastic or non-shrink cement mortar to prevent surface infiltration.
  4. Exposed rock faces checked for hollow drum sounds (rock detachment indicators).
