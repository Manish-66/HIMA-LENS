# CIRIA C551 & C765: Conceptual Ground Models & Risk Registers
### External Standard 4 for Technical Writing & Engineering Reporting

Sourced from the Construction Industry Research and Information Association (CIRIA, United Kingdom), including **CIRIA Report C551** (*Manual on Slope Instability in Soil and Rock*) and **CIRIA C765** (*Geotechnical Risk Management*).

---

## 1. The Conceptual Ground Model (CGM) Principle

CIRIA dictates that **no numerical or limit-equilibrium calculation can be valid without an explicit Conceptual Ground Model (CGM)** established and articulated in the report.

### The Four Pillars of a CGM:
1. **Geomorphological Framework:** Slope gradient, slope aspect, catchment catchment area, relict landslide morphology, natural vs cut slope transitions.
2. **Stratigraphical & Lithological Profile:** Vertical sequence of strata, depth of unconsolidated colluvium, weathering grade (Grade I Fresh Bedrock to Grade VI Residual Soil), discontinuity orientations (bedding, foliation, persistent joint sets).
3. **Hydrogeological Regime:** Surface catchments, infiltration pathways, perennial vs ephemeral springs, transient perched groundwater tables, and seepage points along the cut face.
4. **Anthropogenic / Man-Made Triggers:** Toe steepening due to road cut widening, uncontrolled culvert discharge, surcharge loading from roadside structures, or failure of legacy unreinforced masonry walls.

## 2. Geotechnical Risk Register (GRR) Standard Architecture

CIRIA requires that technical reports summarize slope hazards in a standardized **Geotechnical Risk Register (GRR)**. This table must appear in Section 6 (*Risk Assessment*) of the Preliminary Project Report:

| Risk ID | Hazard Event & Trigger | Primary Receptor | Pre-Mitigation Likelihood ($L$) | Pre-Mitigation Severity ($S$) | Initial Risk Score ($L \times S$) | Prescribed Mitigation Measure | Post-Mitigation Risk Score | Action Owner |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **R-01** | Rapid debris flow encroaching carriageway during monsoon cloudburst ($>60\text{mm/h}$) | Traffic vehicles & passengers | 4 (Likely) | 5 (Critical) | **20 (High)** | Flexible ring-net barrier (1500 kJ) + crest catchwater drain | **4 (Low)** | HPPWD Division Thalout |
| **R-02** | Deep rotational failure in saturated colluvium beneath road formation | Foundation of NH corridor | 3 (Possible) | 4 (Major) | **12 (Medium)** | Cast-in-place plum concrete breast wall on bedrock + sub-horizontal drains | **3 (Low)** | IIT Mandi SCENE / Contractor |
| **R-03** | Rockfall detachment along persistent adverse foliation joint ($42^\circ/210^\circ$) | Single lane traffic & pedestrians | 4 (Likely) | 3 (Moderate) | **12 (Medium)** | Pinned high-tensile wire mesh ($1770\text{ MPa}$) with self-drilling rock bolts | **2 (Low)** | Maintenance Sub-Division |

### Scoring Matrix Definitions:
- **Likelihood ($L$):** 1 (Remote $<5\%$ in 50 yrs), 2 (Unlikely $5-20\%$), 3 (Possible $20-50\%$), 4 (Likely $50-80\%$), 5 (Almost Certain $>80\%$).
- **Severity ($S$):** 1 (Insignificant — debris cleared in 1 hr), 2 (Minor — single-lane restriction $<6$ hrs), 3 (Moderate — full road closure $6-24$ hrs, non-structural damage), 4 (Major — road breach $>24$ hrs, heavy infrastructure destruction, serious injury), 5 (Catastrophic — multiple fatalities, long-term corridor disruption $>7$ days).
- **Risk Evaluation:**
  - $1 - 6$: **Low Risk** (Acceptable under routine maintenance).
  - $8 - 12$: **Medium Risk** (Tolerable only with formal engineering remediation schedule).
  - $15 - 25$: **High / Unacceptable Risk** (Mandatory immediate emergency intervention and traffic control).
