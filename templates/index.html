01 / KINEMATIC DETACHMENT
Crown Tension Fissures & Detachment Scarp
USGS Circular 1325 • Cruden-Varnes (1996)

Gravitational stress relief creates sub-vertical extensional tension cracks 5m to 15m uphill of the cut slope crest. When fissure aperture exceeds 15mm under rainfall saturation, progressive shear rupture initiates kinematic downhill displacement.

Diagnostic Feature: Tension Cracks (>15mm aperture)
Failure Kinematics: Translational Planar Slip
Prescribed Action: Crown Unloading & Interceptor Catch Drains
HPSOR Code: MoRTH Section 301 / HPSOR Item 2.1
