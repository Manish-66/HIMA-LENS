GEOTECHNICAL FOUNDATION

A landslide is more than a point.

Every mapped slope failure exists within a dynamic Himalayan catchment. Its stability is governed by slope inclination, bedrock lithology, structural jointing, road cutting, and monsoon precipitation surges.

HIMA-LENS enables multi-layer geotechnical synthesis — moving from raw incident coordinates to kinematic failure classification and limit-equilibrium stability analysis.

01
WHERE • Spatial & Slope Geometry

Spatial clustering across critical slope-angle bands (35°–55°) and highway toe-excavations.

02
WHEN • Monsoon Precipitation Thresholds

72-hour antecedent rainfall triggers and seasonal saturation curves across historical monsoon events.

03
WHAT • Varnes Kinematics & Remediation

Cruden & Varnes failure modes (rockfall, debris slide, rotational slump) and HPPWD civil remediation schedules.
