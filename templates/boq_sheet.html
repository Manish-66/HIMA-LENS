OFFICIAL STATUTORY NOTICE • HPPWD / MoRTH SPECIFICATIONS:
All unit rates referenced in this dossier are extracted strictly from the Himachal Pradesh Public Works Department (HPPWD) Schedule of Rates for Hill Roads and the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) Standard Data Book for Road & Bridge Works (5th Revision). Derived quantities are computed deterministically from empirical terrain dimensions, surveyed slope scarp geometry, cut angles, and geotechnical stability criteria (IRC:SP:48 / CEDD Geoguide 1). This abstract estimate is prepared for administrative sanction, emergency relief allocation, and SDRF/NDRF financial requisition under the Disaster Management Act 2005.
