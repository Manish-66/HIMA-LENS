HIMA·LENS

Himachal Landslide Inventory & Spatial Intelligence Explorer

A comprehensive decision-support platform designed to enhance mountain hazard resilience across Himachal Pradesh. This state-of-the-art system integrates geological inventories, climate and precipitation data, advanced spatial analytics, and user-friendly exploration tools to help decision-makers, researchers, disaster authorities, and local communities understand and respond to slope-failure risks effectively.

Developed at the School of Civil & Environmental Engineering (SCENE), IIT Mandi, the platform serves diverse users from local community members to government officials, urban planners, disaster management authorities, and geotechnical experts — enabling informed, climate-smart decisions for a resilient Western Himalaya.

SCENE • IIT Mandi Geotechnical & Spatial Intelligence Western Himalaya Decision Support System

Our Mission

HIMA-LENS's mission is to democratize landslide hazard and slope-instability information at all levels of Himalayan society. We strive to bridge the gap between complex geotechnical science, remote sensing, and practical disaster mitigation by providing accessible, user-friendly tools and information.

Our goal is to enable both local communities and decision-makers to better understand, prepare for, and respond to slope failures, ultimately contributing to a climate-resilient Himachal Pradesh.

Our Vision

To be Himachal Pradesh's leading open-access platform for accessible landslide hazard intelligence and decision support, fostering climate-resilient mountain communities through knowledge-driven action.

“Empowering Himalayan communities and infrastructure planners with the knowledge and spatial tools they need to face geotechnical challenges and build a sustainable, hazard-resilient future.”

Technology & Innovation

HIMA-LENS leverages cutting-edge web-GIS and spatial processing technologies to visualize complex geological data for informed decision-making.

Key Features

  • Location-Specific Telemetry:

    Generate detailed slope and hazard profile records for any mapped location in Himachal Pradesh.

  • Multi-Attribute Query Engine:

    Filter data across administrative districts by trigger mechanism, activity state, and failure classification.

  • Intuitive Visualizations:

    View easy-to-understand interactive map layers and responsive point clustering.

Data Sources

Our platform integrates verified landslide data from multiple scientific sources:

  • Geological Baseline Inventories:

    Over 10,593 mapped slope-failure locations across the Western Himalaya.

  • Detailed Attributed Records:

    Granular lithology, hydrology, movement classification, and triggering mechanics for major sites.

  • Standard Geodetic Datum:

    EPSG:4326 / WGS 84 coordinate reference system.
