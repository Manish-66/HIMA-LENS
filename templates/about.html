HIMA·LENS

Himachal Landslide Inventory

A comprehensive decision-support platform designed to enhance mountain hazard resilience across Himachal Pradesh. This state-of-the-art system integrates 14,190 verified slope-failure records, multi-perspective optical reconnaissance, transient precipitation modeling, and semi-automated geotechnical AI assessments to empower disaster authorities, highway engineers, researchers, and local communities.

Developed at the Indian Institute of Technology Mandi (IIT Mandi), the platform bridges field reconnaissance with statutory geotechnical standards — enabling informed, climate-smart decisions for a resilient Western Himalaya.

IIT Mandi Geotechnical Landslide Inventory Western Himalaya Decision Support System

Our Mission

HIMA-LENS's mission is to democratize landslide hazard and slope-instability intelligence at all levels of Himalayan society. We strive to bridge the gap between complex geotechnical science, remote sensing, and practical public works remediation by providing accessible, survey-grade tools and transparent scientific records.

Our goal is to enable both local communities and infrastructure engineers to better understand, prepare for, and remediate slope failures, directly contributing to a climate-resilient Himachal Pradesh.

Our Vision

To serve as Himachal Pradesh's authoritative open-access platform for accessible landslide hazard intelligence, geotechnical kinematic diagnostics, and statutory disaster decision support through science-grounded action.

“Empowering Himalayan communities and highway infrastructure planners with the empirical data and geotechnical modeling tools needed to mitigate slope hazards and build a sustainable future.”

Key Objectives

Our core objectives focus on enhancing mountain disaster resilience, empirical slope cataloging, and statutory geotechnical remediation across Himachal Pradesh.

Empirical Inventory

Catalog 14,190 verified slope failures with granular 10m survey telemetry, geological attributes, and highway proximity across all 12 districts.

Multi-Photo Reconnaissance

Integrate multi-perspective visual evidence (up to 4 field photos) capturing crown scarps, flanks, displaced colluvial mass, and road carriageways.

Statutory Compliance

Enforce Indian geotechnical engineering standards (MoRTH, IRC:SP:48, IS:14458, IS:1200, HPSOR 2024-25) in civil mitigation recommendations.

Kinematic Diagnostics

Generate real-time 2D geotechnical cross-sections visualizing slip surfaces, phreatic seepage, colluvial volumes, and stabilization structures.

Science to Practice

Translate advanced limit equilibrium stability equations (Bishop LEQ) into actionable Level-1 engineering reconnaissance dossiers for PWD engineers.

Technology & Innovation

HIMA-LENS leverages cutting-edge Web-GIS, limit equilibrium geomechanics, and multimodal computer vision to transform slope hazard reconnaissance into actionable engineering dossiers.

Key Capabilities

  • Multi-Photo Evidence Intake:

    Upload and analyze up to 4 high-resolution field photos per incident, processed by multimodal VLM models and rendered on adaptive A4 plates.

  • Kinematic Cross-Section Engine:

    Interactive 2D schematic SVG generator dynamically depicting slope geometry, slip arcs, phreatic lines, and retaining wall configurations.

  • Statutory PWD Field Dossiers:

    Automated Level-1 engineering reconnaissance sheets adhering strictly to MoRTH, IRC:SP:48, and IS:14458 specifications.

Data Architecture

Our platform integrates verified landslide data from multiple scientific sources:

  • Empirical Spatial Inventory:

    14,190 mapped slope-failure locations across all 12 districts of Himachal Pradesh (including 9,399 detailed 10m survey records in Mandi and 4,791 statewide GSI locations).

  • Infrastructure Corridors:

    2,287 Mandi road segments and 17,122 landslide polygons referenced to NH-21, NH-05, and state highway alignments.

  • Standard Geodetic Datum:

    EPSG:4326 / WGS 84 coordinate reference system with 3D elevation drapes.
